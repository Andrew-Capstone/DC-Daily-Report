Microsoft unveils third-generation Maia 300 AI accelerator, claiming 40% more tokens per megawatt

TechEnergy

Microsoft unveiled Maia 300 on October 12 as an inference-first accelerator for long-context, memory-intensive workloads. It claims 40% more tokens per megawatt than leading GPU systems and up to seven times the rack-scale throughput of Maia 200 in a 96-accelerator rack. Production deployment is slated for 2027.

Why it matters: Power-efficiency gains in custom silicon bear directly on how much AI capacity a fixed grid connection can serve.
