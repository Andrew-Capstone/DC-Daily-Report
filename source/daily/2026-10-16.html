1

NERC reliability standards for AI data centres and crypto miners clear stakeholder vote

PolicyEnergy

Stakeholders approved NERC Project 2026-02 on Oct. 15, setting reliability rules for computational loads of 50 MW or more connected at 100 kV or higher. The standards cover interconnection studies, operational data and load-protection monitoring, and go next to NERC's board (expected in Q4) and then FERC.

Why it matters: A continent-wide rulebook for data centre grid behaviour follows incidents where sites dropped load suddenly; NERC projects 224 GW of summer peak growth over a decade.

Google's 1 GW Michigan campus wins final site plan approval; DTE signs 20-year power contract

ConstructionEnergy

Van Buren Township's planning commission approved the 282-acre Project Cannoli site plan 5-1. Google expects power draw to begin in December 2027; its 20-year DTE contract includes 480 MW of storage and 1.6 GW of renewables, and regulators approved it Oct. 1.

Why it matters: Shows how a hyperscaler is funding new supply to land a gigawatt site, even as local opposition and a recall effort grow.

Why AI data centre cooling needs to get smarter: 100 kW racks, 48 V fans and 800 V HVDC

Tech

Why it matters: Cooling and power distribution are being redesigned together for megawatt-class racks.
