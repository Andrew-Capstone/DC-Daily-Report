Amazon and Constellation sign 20-year, 690 MW nuclear deal to upgrade Calvert Cliffs

EnergyPolicy

Constellation and Amazon signed a 20-year power purchase agreement for 690 MW from Maryland's Calvert Cliffs nuclear plant, including 190 MW of uprates due between 2030 and 2032. Constellation says it will plan about $3 billion of improvements and that the contract supports relicensing the plant for another 20 years.

Why it matters: Another hyperscaler locking in firm nuclear output, with new capacity added through uprates rather than new reactors.

Duke Energy and tech giants agree on large-load tariff for North Carolina data centres

PolicyEnergy

Duke reached a settlement with data centre operators on a special rate for big new energy users. Reported terms include a 50 MW trigger and 75% minimum billing; the tariff would not apply to customers contracted on or before June 1, 2026, and the N.C. Utilities Commission must still approve it. Environmental groups say it falls short on cost allocation and transmission upgrades.

Why it matters: Large-load tariffs are becoming the template for who pays for the grid build-out behind AI campuses.
BPR → (Oct 7)

Texas data centre interconnection pause: TCEQ permitting update due Oct. 19

PolicyEnergy

Gov. Greg Abbott ordered a pause on data centre grid connections pending a PUC/ERCOT audit of a queue of about 474 GW, roughly 90% of it data centres. Fox reports the TCEQ is due to update its permitting decisions by Oct. 19. BNEF estimates the pause puts about 20% of the US pipeline at risk of delay.

Why it matters: The largest US data centre market is on hold, with a decision point days away; sources differ on some dates.

Why AI data centre cooling needs to get smarter: 100 kW racks, 48 V fans and 800 V HVDC

Tech

AI workloads are pushing rack power past 100 kW, with some clusters expected to exceed 1 MW within a few years. Gallium nitride devices can lift pump efficiency by up to 2%, server fans are moving from 12 V to 48 V, and coolant pumps will need to support 800 V HVDC.

Why it matters: Cooling and power distribution are being redesigned together for megawatt-class racks.

Why it matters: Grid access, not location, now sets land value.
