1

Google signs 3.6 GW Constellation power deal in PJM, including 890 MW of nuclear uprates

EnergyFinance

Google contracted 3,590 MW from Constellation Energy in PJM, announced October 6. A 20-year PPA covers 890 MW from upgrades at 11 nuclear units in Illinois, Pennsylvania and New Jersey, with first power in 2028, plus a separate 2,700 MW supply deal not tied to a specific source. The deal enables more than $4.3 billion of new Constellation investment.

Why it matters: Hyperscalers are buying capacity ahead of PJM's proposed bring-your-own-power rules.
Times Live (Reuters) → (Oct 6)
2

Black Hills signs definitive agreements to power Google's Cheyenne campus with $1.8B of new generation

EnergyConstruction

The agreements, effective September 30 and running through 2048, have Black Hills provide up to 590 MW of grid-connected service and manage about 2.1 GW of third-party Wyoming resources through a private microgrid. It will spend $1.8 billion from 2027 to 2029 on 564 MW of company-owned generation, with service starting late 2027 and peak load in 2030.

Why it matters: A utility-built, ring-fenced power package is becoming the model for gigawatt campuses.
SEC Form 8-K exhibit (Black Hills) → (Oct 6)

Bell to quadruple Saskatchewan AI data centre to 1.2 GW, with investment that could top $50B

CanadaEnergyFinance

Announced September 14 at the Canada Investment Summit, the plan adds up to 900 MW under Saskatchewan's Bring Your Own Power policy to the original 300 MW near Regina. BetaKit reports the expansion is a non-binding MOU, and the existing capacity is pre-sold to Cerebras and CoreWeave.

Why it matters: It is Canada's largest announced sovereign AI project, though still a commitment in principle.
BetaKit → (Sep 14)
4

Texas governor orders TCEQ to freeze all data centre permits until ERCOT audit completes

PolicyEnergy

Governor Abbott's September 21 order covers all data centre projects statewide, including islanded sites using behind-the-meter power. The pause runs until ERCOT finishes its audit, expected mid-December, and TCEQ must report compliance by October 19. Abbott wants projects to cover grid costs, lower residential bills and report water use.

Why it matters: The largest data centre state is effectively on hold through year-end.
Bracewell LLP → (Sep 21-23)
5

PJM asks FERC to require new data centres to bring their own power, with an order requested by Oct 12

PolicyEnergy

Under the proposal, new large loads that have not brought their own power would be first to be moved to backup generators or curtailed on the hottest and coldest days. PJM asked FERC to act by October 12, and Ohio regulators argue the definition should be limited to data centres and exclude manufacturers.

Why it matters: A FERC decision this week could reshape power procurement across 13 states.
Virginia Mercury via 12 On Your Side → (Aug 27 (decision pending))

Google details grid-interactive AI campuses and a move to 800 VDC

TechEnergy

At Data Center World Power, Google's Tom Garvens said compute demand grew sevenfold from May 2025 to May 2026, and Google is moving from centralized UPS to rack-level 54 VDC batteries and gradually to 800 VDC. Racks are expected to approach 1 MW within one to two years, and Google can shift non-critical workloads during grid events.

Why it matters: Hyperscalers are redesigning power systems so campuses act as grid partners.

Energy

Why it matters: Access to power, more than capital, is the constraint on the build-out.
