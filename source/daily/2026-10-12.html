1

Google signs 20-year nuclear PPA with Constellation for 890 MW across 11 PJM reactors

Energy

Google will buy 890 MW of new nuclear output from Constellation under a 20-year agreement covering 11 reactors in the PJM Interconnection. Constellation plans to spend over $4.3 billion on reactor upgrades.

Why it matters: One of the largest hyperscaler nuclear contracts to date, adding firm carbon-free supply in the most constrained grid region.

AWS files 36-building plan at 4.5 GW gas-powered Homer City campus in Pennsylvania

ConstructionEnergy

Amazon Data Services filed a preliminary land development plan for 36 buildings on the former Homer City coal plant site, to be powered by a gas plant of roughly 4.4 to 4.5 GW. Construction is slated to begin in December with completion targeted for 2036, though the county planning commission tabled the application after more than 100 residents raised noise, water and boundary concerns.

Why it matters: A hyperscaler is anchoring one of the largest gas-powered campuses in North America, with local opposition already surfacing.

Google details grid-interactive AI data centres, from backup to grid partner

TechEnergy

Google says it processes more than 3 quadrillion AI tokens per month, a sevenfold rise in compute demand from May 2025 to May 2026. It uses battery storage to buffer fluctuating workloads, has moved to rack-level 54 VDC battery backup and is migrating toward 800 VDC, and works with Meta, Microsoft and Nvidia on a utility interface specification.

Why it matters: Shows how the largest operators plan to turn load volatility into a grid-support tool.
