1

Amazon signs 20-year nuclear PPA with Constellation for Maryland's Calvert Cliffs plant

Energy

Constellation and Amazon agreed a 20-year power purchase agreement covering the plant's 1,790 MW existing output plus 190 MW of new capacity from uprates planned for 2030-2032. More than $3 billion is earmarked for upgrades, and the deal supports relicensing the plant for another 20 years.

Why it matters: Another hyperscaler locks up existing nuclear baseload, following Constellation's deals with Meta and Microsoft.
EnergyTech → (Oct 1)
2

Valar Atomics proposes 456-reactor, 9.6 GW nuclear-powered AI campus in central Utah

EnergyTech

'Project Beehive' near Price would combine data centres with 456 small reactors, exceeding Utah's current average energy output. Utah's trust lands agency approved a lease on 640+ acres on Sept 17 and the BLM is reviewing an application for about 9,000 acres, with NEPA review and public comment still ahead.

Why it matters: The most ambitious SMR-data-centre concept yet, though no company has commercially operated an SMR and permitting is early.
KSL → (Sept 30)
3

Enerflex wins 450 MW natural-gas generator order from a North American data-centre developer

EnergyCanada

The Calgary-based equipment maker will design and build 450 MW of gas-fired generators at its North American plants, with deliveries from 2027 to 2028. Financial terms were not disclosed, and Enerflex shares jumped about 15% in Toronto.

Why it matters: Off-grid prime power is becoming a standard route for data centres that cannot wait for utility interconnection.
EnergyNow → (Oct 1)
4

Google signs record 396 MW geothermal PPA with Fervo for a Utah data centre

EnergyTech

Fervo will supply power from its Cape Station enhanced geothermal project for a potential Google data centre in Utah by June 2030, with an option to expand by about 600 MW toward 1 GW. Search results also report Fervo reached first power at Cape Station in late September (details from search summaries).

Why it matters: The largest enhanced-geothermal PPA to date shows firm clean power beyond nuclear is becoming bankable for AI load.

CanadaConstructionFinance

Canada launches Responsible Data Centre Development Principles with 23 industry signatories

CanadaPolicy

Minister Evan Solomon unveiled five principles: local benefits, ratepayer protection, minimal water and environmental impact, transparency, and strategic value to Canada. Signatories include AWS, Google, Microsoft, Meta, OpenAI and Anthropic.

Why it matters: A national baseline for community expectations arrives as projects scale into the gigawatts.

7

Google details grid-interactive AI data centres and a move to 800 VDC power

TechEnergy

Google's Tom Garvens said the company uses batteries and demand-response agreements for peak shaving, is moving to rack-level 54 VDC backup and plans 800 VDC distribution within about two years, gaining roughly 1.5% efficiency. Google now processes over 3 quadrillion AI tokens a month.

Why it matters: Data centres are shifting from grid burdens to grid partners, while power architecture is being redesigned for AI.
Data Center Knowledge → (Sept 25)
8

Supercapacitors emerge as a fix for AI power swings, with market seen at $950M by 2037

Tech

AI compute events can spike loads past 150% of a power shelf's rating; supercapacitors react in milliseconds but sustain output for only about 2 to 10 minutes. IDTechEx projects the data-centre segment growing 40.9% a year, though the market is still at pilot and early commercial stage.

Why it matters: They complement rather than replace UPS and batteries, adding a new layer to AI power design.
Data Center Knowledge → (Sept 28)

FinanceConstruction

Why it matters: Contracted demand is outrunning financing, power and permitting, which is now the real bottleneck.

Why it matters: The world's largest data-centre market is making approval slower and more political.

Senate stalls Ratepayer Protection Act as Virginia, Loudoun tighten rules

PolicyEnergy

After the House passed H.R. 9340 417-3 on Sept 17, the Senate blocked passage a day later amid a dispute between Sen. Heinrich and Sen. Moreno. Loudoun County voted to pause new approvals for up to 12 months, and roughly 120 projects worth about $198B faced opposition in H1 2026.

Why it matters: The federal ratepayer fix is stuck while local limits multiply.
