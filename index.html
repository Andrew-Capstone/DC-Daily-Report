Microsoft unveils Maia 300 AI chip, claims 40% more tokens per megawatt than top GPU setups

Tech

Microsoft revealed its third-generation Maia accelerator on Oct. 12, slated for production in 2027, and said Maia 200 is now in production in US, European and Australian data centres. It claims Maia 200 delivers up to 1.7 times the tokens per watt of leading GPU infrastructure, and Maia 300 up to seven times Maia 200's rack-scale throughput.

Why it matters: Custom silicon aimed at power efficiency targets the binding constraint on AI capacity; Microsoft plans multi-gigawatt Maia deployments in 2027 but has not opened Maia to Azure customers.

NERC reliability standards for AI data centres and crypto miners clear stakeholder vote

PolicyEnergy

McKinsey: data centre power demand to grow 24% a year through 2030

Energy

McKinsey's Global Energy Perspective 2026 projects a 24% compound annual growth rate in data centre electricity demand through 2030. A McKinsey partner said its most likely scenarios all have power supply lower than IT compute demand, and that over 60% of operators now combine on-site generation with grid access.

Why it matters: Power availability, not chips, will determine how much compute comes online, and gas pipeline competition could delay new generation.

EnergyTech

Why it matters: Data centres acting as flexible grid partners rather than passive loads is a key route to faster interconnection.
