Google signs 20-year nuclear PPA with Constellation for 890 MW across 11 PJM reactors

Energy

Announced Oct 6, the contract has Constellation investing over $4.3B in upgrades at plants in Illinois, Pennsylvania and New Jersey, with the first output increase in 2028. A separate 15-year deal covers 2,700 MW from existing PJM plants, for roughly 3.6 GW combined.

Why it matters: One of the largest hyperscaler nuclear contracts to date, adding firm carbon-free supply in the grid region most constrained by data centre demand.

2

Valar Atomics files for 9.4 GW SMR-powered data centre campus in Utah

EnergyTech

Project Beehive covers more than 9,000 acres of BLM land near Price, Utah, with 456 small modular reactors, fuel manufacturing and waste storage. The first reactor is targeted for 2028 and completion for 2032. The filing follows the Ward 250 test reactor reaching criticality four months ago, and some residents object.

Why it matters: An ambitious on-site SMR concept that would dwarf current plans, though it still depends on federal approvals and unproven deployment.

3

Bell's Saskatchewan AI data centre quadruples to about 1.2 GW and $50B+

CanadaEnergy

Announced Sept 14 at the Canada Investment Summit, the Regina-area project keeps its original 300 MW from the provincial grid and adds up to 900 MW that Bell supplies itself under Saskatchewan's Bring Your Own Power policy. The agreement is a non-binding MOU, and the province cites up to 500 permanent jobs.

Why it matters: Canada's largest announced sovereign AI project, and a test of the bring-your-own-power model.

Alberta pitches surplus power as Meta's $13B+ Sturgeon County campus advances

CanadaEnergy

Calgary.Tech reports Meta's project is backed by the 932 MW Greenlight gas plant, due in service in the second half of 2030, and a 250 MW Capital Power supply deal starting in the second half of 2028. Alberta has about 1.2 GW of surplus generation, and Meta expects about 3,000 trades workers at peak.

Why it matters: Shows how gas-backed supply is shaping where Canada's AI campuses are built.

Nvidia's Vera Rubin racks push 100% liquid cooling and 800 V DC power

Tech

DCD reports the NVL144 rack design uses 45°C liquid cooling and an 800-volt DC architecture, which Nvidia says carries over 150% more power through the same copper. Vera Rubin is slated for the second half of 2026, with Kyber racks in 2027. This coverage dates from the GTC announcements earlier this year.

Why it matters: Operators designing new halls now have to plan for liquid cooling and DC distribution rather than treat them as options.

AI power spikes force chip-to-grid design changes, panelists warn

TechEnergy

At Data Center World Power in Dallas, panelists said AI loads swing in milliseconds while grid generation takes minutes to ramp. Nvidia has added rack-level storage and controls to smooth swings, and Oracle is exploring smaller on-site generation blocks in Abilene.

Why it matters: Load volatility is becoming a grid-stability issue that chip makers, operators and utilities must solve together.
