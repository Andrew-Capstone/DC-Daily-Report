Anthropic signs first Canadian data centre lease with EverWind at Point Tupper, Nova Scotia

CanadaEnergy

Anthropic signed a lease of at least 20 years for capacity at EverWind's Point Tupper campus, starting with 95 MW in late 2027 and growing to 420 MW with a further 325 MW in 2028. EverWind plans more than C$10 billion in total, including battery storage and funding of grid upgrades; the provincial NDP leader criticized the lack of public consultation.

Why it matters: The first major Canadian AI campus pairs wind and storage with a hyperscale tenant, and is already a test of consultation and ratepayer protection.

Nvidia unveils DSX Pod, a prebuilt AI data centre module that cuts deployment to as little as 14 days

Tech

The containerized pod ships with seven Vera Rubin NVL72 racks plus networking, cabling and cooling installed, versus about six months for a conventional build at a prepared site. It was announced at GTC Berlin with 17 partners including Dell, HPE, Lenovo, Schneider Electric and Vertiv, for availability in early 2027.

Why it matters: Factory-integrated AI capacity could compress build timelines wherever power is already available, including North America.

PolicyEnergy
