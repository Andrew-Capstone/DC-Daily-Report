AI data centres are fueling a nuclear comeback, with about $68B of new plant spending expected through 2029

Energy

Industrial Info Resource estimates new nuclear project spending at about $20 billion in 2026, rising to nearly $50 billion by 2029, across more than 100 North American projects (61 involving SMRs). DOE offered Constellation a loan of up to $5 billion for uprates at eight plants, and Amazon, Microsoft and Google all have nuclear arrangements in the US.

Why it matters: Ontario and Alberta together account for roughly $40 billion of the tracked spend, putting Canada at the centre of the nuclear build-out.

2

Crusoe debuts Spark Liquid, a 3.5 MW factory-built liquid-cooled modular unit

TechConstruction

Crusoe's sixth-generation modular platform supports Nvidia GB300 and Vera Rubin NVL72 racks, with closed-loop direct liquid cooling and what the company says is five times the compute density of earlier Spark generations. Production joins its Brighton, Colorado factory in 2027; Nvidia notes customers still need a prepared site with power, water and network.

Why it matters: Factory-built capacity is one answer to construction delays, though grid interconnection remains the gating item.
Source → (Data Center Knowledge (Nov 4))
3

California Resources and Bloom Energy sign MOU to evaluate 1 GW of fuel cells with carbon capture

Energy

The non-binding memorandum covers at least 1 GW of solid-oxide fuel cell generation in California, with projects that could make up to 3 million tonnes a year of CO2 available for capture and storage. Data centres are named among potential offtakers, and projects depend on definitive agreements, a final investment decision and EPA Class VI permits.

Why it matters: It pairs on-site firm power with carbon capture, an option for data centres facing grid constraints and emissions scrutiny.
Source → (Baxtel (Nov 3))
4

Trump's on-site power push meets AI data centre reality

EnergyPolicy

In an Oct 21 post, President Trump said large new data centres should generate their own electricity and share surplus with the local grid. Data Center Knowledge notes some gas turbine models are reportedly sold out through 2031, and experts expect most sites to use a hybrid of on-site generation, storage and grid supply.

Why it matters: A federal lean toward self-powered campuses could force projects secured on grid or PPA power to rethink their plans.
Source → (Data Center Knowledge (Nov 3))

SpaceXAI adds two Southaven, Mississippi sites to Colossus 2, with about 420,000 more GPUs planned

ConstructionEnergy

Elon Musk said the 'Gun Range' site (about 220,000 GB300 GPUs) should be operational by end of November and 'Spam' (about 200,000, for inference) in December. Power relies mainly on gas turbines; a federal judge on Oct 30 denied a motion to dismiss the NAACP's Clean Air Act lawsuit, and contractors have filed more than $220 million in liens.

Why it matters: One of the fastest and most contested AI build-outs in the US, testing both gas-fired power and permitting.
