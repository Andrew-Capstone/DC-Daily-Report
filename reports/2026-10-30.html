Google named as user behind 1,200 MW Goochland, Virginia campus

ConstructionEnergy

Goochland County confirmed on October 28 that Google is the customer for a roughly 1,000-acre, 1,200 MW campus in West Creek Business Park. Google is doing due diligence on the land, and the project holds four engineering letters of authorization with Dominion Energy, placing it in the utility's queue ahead of a feasibility study.

Why it matters: A gigawatt-class Google campus in Dominion territory will test how much new load Virginia's grid can take.

Tech

Microsoft claims about 40% more tokens per megawatt than leading GPU systems and plans multi-gigawatt Maia deployment in 2027. EnergyConstruction

Clayco and Kiewit topped out the first 855,000 sq ft building of the Doña Ana County, New Mexico campus leased by Oracle for OpenAI. On-site Bloom Energy fuel cells replace gas turbines and diesel generators in the design, cutting nitrogen oxide emissions by about 92%, according to the article.

Why it matters: A multi-chip cloud could ease Nvidia dependence and shift data centre design.

9

xMEMS begins mass production of solid-state micro-cooling chips

Tech

xMEMS is in mass production of its XMC-2400 and XMC-4800 silicon chips, which move air without blades or bearings to target hotspots in SSDs, memory, retimers and optics that cold plates miss. The company positions them as a complement to rack-level cooling.

Why it matters: Hotspot cooling for components beyond the GPU is a growing thermal bottleneck.
Source → (Data Center Knowledge (Oct 28))
