Texas governor-elect pledges to pause new data centre approvals until lawmakers adopt rules

Policy

Governor-elect Gina Hinojosa wants to halt new approvals until the Legislature passes new rules, and her plan would require union labour, bar NDAs and end subsidies. Gov. Greg Abbott already ordered an audit of data centres in the ERCOT queue, which holds over 474 GW of requests, about 90% from data centres; the audit is expected to conclude in December.

Why it matters: Texas holds roughly 25% of planned US data centre capacity, so a pause would reshape the continent's biggest pipeline.

Voters back local data centre limits in 40 of 41 ballot measures across 10 states

Policy

Per Ballotpedia, voters in 10 states backed limits or rejected pro-development proposals in 40 of 41 local measures on Nov. 3; Ohio alone had 21. A CNBC poll found 56% of voters called data centres a crucial or major factor in their congressional vote.

Why it matters: Local opposition has moved from a siting nuisance to an electoral force that developers must now plan around.

Google backstops TeraWulf debt for $19B Anthropic campus in Kentucky

FinanceConstruction

Google will take responsibility for project debt if TeraWulf defaults on its Hawesville, Kentucky campus, which carries a roughly $19 billion, 20-year Anthropic lease. Fluor signed an EPC contract Nov. 4 for three buildings, with completions from late 2027 to Q2 2028 on a site with approved power of up to 482 MW.

Why it matters: Hyperscaler credit support is becoming the financing template for AI campuses built by non-traditional developers.
