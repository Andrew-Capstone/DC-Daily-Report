Supermicro begins shipping Nvidia Vera Rubin NVL72 racks with 1.8 MW liquid-cooling units

Tech

Supermicro has started shipping NVIDIA Vera Rubin NVL72 racks built on its DLC-2 direct liquid cooling and 1.8 MW in-row coolant distribution units with N+1 redundancy. Each rack holds 72 Rubin GPUs and 36 Vera CPUs with 20.7 TB of HBM4, and a 16-rack scalable unit spans 1,152 GPUs and 331 TB of HBM4.

Why it matters: Fully liquid-cooled rack-scale systems are moving from announcement to delivery, setting the cooling and power design baseline for the next wave of AI halls.

Bell's Saskatchewan AI data centre quadruples to 1.2 GW, drawing a third protest in the RM of Sherwood

CanadaPolicy

Dozens of protesters gathered at the RM of Sherwood office near Regina over Bell's expanded AI data centre, which would grow from 300 MW to as much as 1.2 GW under a roughly $52 billion plan. Concerns raised include environmental impact, whether Regina's electrical infrastructure can cope, and the transparency of the approval process.

Why it matters: Canada's flagship sovereign-AI campus now faces organised local opposition as its scale quadruples.

Meta breaks ground on a 1 GW, C$13 billion AI data centre in Sturgeon County, Alberta, backed by gas generation

CanadaEnergyConstruction

Meta's first Canadian data centre is a 1 GW AI-optimized campus with an investment above C$13 billion, about 3,000 construction workers at peak and more than 300 operational jobs. Meta says it will add enough clean energy to Alberta's grid to match its use and is funding new generation and grid infrastructure, with gas-fired power supplied by Pembina's $3.2 billion Greenlight Electricity Centre, slated for the end of 2030. Note: announced in July; included as the anchor Canadian build.

Why it matters: It is the largest announced AI data centre investment in Canada and ties it directly to new Alberta gas capacity.

NRG aligns on a 1.2 GW gas plant for a hyperscaler in Texas under a 'bring your own power' model

EnergyFinance

NRG Energy said it has aligned on principal commercial terms with an unnamed hyperscaler for a 1.2 GW combined-cycle gas plant in Texas, with delivery planned for late 2029. The customer backs the plant through long-term capacity payments, which are expected to supply more than 95% of cash flow, and NRG's CEO said Governor Abbott's interconnection pause would not impede the project. Note: reported in August.

Why it matters: Customer-funded dedicated generation is becoming the template for getting power to AI load faster.

House passes Ratepayer Protection Act 417-3 to make data centres pay for grid upgrades

PolicyEnergy

The House passed the bipartisan Ratepayer Protection Act by 417 to 3 under a two-thirds fast-track vote. It aims to require tech companies to pay for new energy infrastructure serving data centres rather than shifting costs to consumers, and it now goes to the Senate.

Why it matters: A near-unanimous vote shows how politically potent electricity bills have become for data-centre growth.

Xcel Energy asks Texas regulators for a large-load tariff making data centres cover grid costs

PolicyEnergy

Xcel wants the Texas PUC to approve a tariff requiring data centres and other large users to pay for the transmission lines and substations needed to serve them. It would impose minimum 15-year commitments with termination fees to prevent stranded costs reaching residential customers.

Why it matters: Long-term commitments and exit fees are becoming standard for large-load customers.

Texas PUC softens large-load interconnection rules, dropping a $50,000-per-megawatt fee

PolicyEnergy

The commission replaced a proposed non-refundable $50,000-per-MW fee with a flat $100,000 study fee for all project sizes. It also extended the window for flagging missed energization milestones from six to 24 months and raised refunds of financial security to the full remaining balance.

Why it matters: A retreat from the March proposal lowers upfront cost for developers in the largest U.S. data-centre market.

11

Virginia's Executive Order 22 bans state data-centre NDAs and sets an accountability framework

Policy

Governor Spanberger's order bars state agencies from confidentiality agreements on data centres and excludes projects of 25 MW or more from state expedited permitting and economic-development programs. It also creates the VA-LEAD environmental designation, accelerated noise rules, cooling-water scarcity designations and a rapid-response AI task force.

Why it matters: The world's densest data-centre market is tightening transparency and incentives, with codification planned for 2027.
Greenberg Traurig → (Sept 18)

New Jersey fines DataOne $1.07 million over 62 unpermitted gas generators at a Vineland data centre

PolicyEnergy

Regulators penalised DataOne for operating 62 natural gas generators without required air permits. Experts note on-site generation eases grid strain but brings local NOx, CO, particulate and noise impacts, and recommend site-level disclosure of equipment, fuel capacity, runtime and emissions.

Why it matters: As operators self-generate to skip the queue, air-permit compliance is becoming a real operating risk.
