Google signs 20-year nuclear PPA with Constellation for 890 MW across 11 PJM reactors

Energy

The Oct 6 deal has Constellation spending over $4.3B on upgrades in Illinois, Pennsylvania and New Jersey. A separate 15-year agreement covers 2,700 MW from existing PJM plants, for about 3.6 GW combined. Reuters reported it responds to PJM's proposal that large loads bring their own generation or face curtailment.

Why it matters: One of the largest hyperscaler nuclear contracts yet, and a template for meeting PJM's bring-your-own-power direction.

AWS files 36-building plan at the 4.5 GW gas-powered Homer City campus in Pennsylvania

EnergyConstruction

Amazon Data Services filed with Indiana County for the former coal-plant site, backed by a planned 4.4 to 4.5 GW gas plant. Construction is slated to begin in December with completion targeted for June 2036. Commissioners tabled the application after more than 100 residents raised noise, water and boundary concerns.

Why it matters: Shows both the scale of behind-the-fence gas-powered campuses and the local pushback they face.

Valar Atomics proposes 9.4 GW SMR-powered data centre campus in Utah

EnergyTech

Project Beehive seeks federal approval for over 9,000 acres of BLM land near Price, Utah, with 456 factory-built small modular reactors. The first reactor is targeted for 2028 and full build-out by 2032. Local residents remain divided over pollution and experimental technology.

Why it matters: An ambitious proposal that would dwarf current SMR plans, though it still depends on federal approvals and unproven deployment.

7

AI power swings force chip-to-grid design changes

TechEnergy

Panelists at Data Center World Power warned that AI workloads can swing hundreds of megawatts in seconds, while many grid turbines need 30 minutes or more to synchronize. Nvidia says rack power targets 240 kW for Vera Rubin and has added rack-level storage to smooth fluctuations. Oracle favours roughly 30 MW building blocks to limit single points of failure.

Why it matters: Load volatility is becoming a grid-reliability issue, not only a capacity one.

McKinsey: data centre power demand to grow 24% a year through 2030

Energy

McKinsey's Global Energy Perspective 2026 says its most likely scenarios leave power supply short of IT compute demand. Constraints include interconnection, permitting, moratoriums, transformers and construction capacity. More than 60% of operators plan to pair on-site generation with grid access.

Why it matters: Power access, not capital, is setting the pace of the build-out.

New Jersey fines DataOne $1.07M over unpermitted generators at Vineland data centre

PolicyEnergy

The state's largest data centre penalty covers 62 unpermitted gas generators at a 300 MW campus being built for Nebius, which a Rutgers researcher estimates emitted about 140 tons of nitrogen oxides. DataOne has 45 days to obtain permits or stop running them, and says it is disputing the determination.

Why it matters: Regulators are now enforcing against on-site power used as a bridge to grid connection.
