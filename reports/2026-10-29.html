6

xMEMS begins mass production of solid-state micro-cooling chips

Tech

xMEMS is in mass production of its XMC-2400 and XMC-4800 silicon chips, which move air without blades or bearings to target hotspots in SSDs, memory, retimers and optics that cold plates miss. The company positions them as a complement to rack-level cooling.

Why it matters: Hotspot cooling for components beyond the GPU is a growing thermal bottleneck.
Source → (Data Center Knowledge (Oct 28))
7

ZutaCore unveils two-phase matrix flow boiling for AI cold plates

Tech

ZutaCore's OmniTherm cold plates use many small boiling regions to hold hot chips at uniform temperature, in a stack under 5 mm thick. The company says its loop needs 0.6 to 0.7 litres per minute per kilowatt versus 1.5 to 2.0 for single-phase cooling, and deployments for Nvidia RTX PRO 6000 Blackwell are underway this quarter. These are company claims.

Why it matters: Two-phase direct-to-chip cooling is moving toward higher-density AI racks with less coolant flow.
Source → (Baxtel (Oct 26))
8

Nvidia unveils DSX POD, a prefabricated Vera Rubin AI data centre module

Tech

The containerized module integrates seven Vera Rubin NVL72 racks with networking, cabling and cooling, and Nvidia says deployment at a prepared site drops from about six months to as little as two weeks. Seventeen partners plan availability in early 2027.

Why it matters: Factory-built pods could compress build timelines, provided power and sites are ready.
Source → (Data Center Knowledge (Oct 21))
