Fervo's Cape Station becomes the world's first commercial enhanced geothermal plant

EnergyTech

The first third of a planned 100 MW plant began selling power to the grid on September 30, 23 months after groundbreaking. Google and Southern California Edison are among the buyers, the site could reach 4 GW, and Fervo aims to build later phases in about 18 months.

Why it matters: Fast, phased, firm clean power matches how data centres ramp, and this is the first proof at commercial scale.

Startup proposes 456-reactor, nuclear-powered data centre campus on Utah public land

EnergyTechPolicy

Valar Atomics' Project Beehive would span more than 9,000 acres of Bureau of Land Management land near Price, Utah, with about 9.6 GW from 25 MW high-temperature gas reactors. Non-construction work could start late this year with first reactors targeted for 2028, but a federal review of water, fuel transport and emergency planning is still ahead.

Why it matters: The most ambitious nuclear-for-AI proposal yet, and a test of whether regulators can keep pace.
NPR → (Sept 30)

Meta's $13B+ Alberta data centre pairs with a 932 MW gas plant and a Capital Power supply deal

CanadaEnergyConstruction

Meta's Sturgeon County campus is Alberta's largest tech infrastructure investment, with operations expected in the second half of 2030. The 932 MW Greenlight Electricity Centre supplies power behind the fence, and Capital Power commits 250 MW from the second half of 2028.

Why it matters: Alberta's bring-your-own-generation model is becoming Canada's template for gigawatt AI campuses.

We Energies strikes deal to power Oracle data center with Point Beach nuclear energy

Energy

Oracle would subscribe to 10% to 20% of Point Beach Nuclear Plant's output for its Port Washington, Wisconsin campus. The utility says other customers could be spared about $300 million in fuel costs over five years; state regulators must still approve.

Why it matters: A nuclear subscription pitched as a ratepayer saver is a template other utilities may copy.
