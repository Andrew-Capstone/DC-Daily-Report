Amazon acquires 8,000-acre Texas site tied to what could be the largest gas plant in the US

EnergyConstruction

Amazon has acquired an 8,000-acre site in Pecos County, West Texas, where it plans to draw power from a 7.65GW natural gas plant being developed by Pacifico Energy using 35 turbines, backed by 1.8GW of battery storage and 750MW of on-site solar. The plant, which just received the largest air permit ever issued in the country (33 million tons of CO2 annually), would run entirely off the Texas grid, with first power targeted for Q1 2027.

Why it matters: It's Amazon's first fully off-grid AI campus and would eclipse every operating gas plant in the country, underscoring how far hyperscalers are now going to bypass slow interconnection queues.

FinanceTechEnergy

Bitcoin miner Riot Platforms agreed to a 20-year, $9.1 billion lease for 191MW of capacity at its Rockdale, Texas campus with a customer Bloomberg reports is Anthropic — Riot's second tenant there after AMD signed on in January. Nautilus cooling unit becomes first liquid-to-liquid CDU validated by NVIDIA, tops DSX directory

Tech

Nautilus Data Technologies' EcoCore Facility Cooling Distribution Unit became the first liquid-to-liquid CDU to achieve NVIDIA Large-Scale CDU validation, now listed on the DSX Marketplace with 4.1MW cooling capacity in dual-pump mode (3.2MW in N+1) — the highest of any system in Nvidia's directory. The water-source-agnostic unit passed over 100 NVIDIA technical requirements and can run on process, chilled, river, sea or wastewater.

Why it matters: As rack densities climb with each new GPU generation, NVIDIA-validated cooling capacity is becoming a gating factor for how fast AI factories can actually be built and certified.

7

Virginia regulators order Dominion Energy to directly bill data centers for transmission costs

Policy

Virginia's utility regulator ordered Dominion Energy to directly assign the transmission infrastructure costs driven by new data center connections to those data centers, rather than spreading them across all ratepayers. Dominion serves the largest concentration of data centers in the world in Northern Virginia's "Data Center Alley."

Why it matters: It's one of the most direct cost-allocation rulings yet in the country's biggest data center market, and a template other state regulators are likely to study closely.

Canada: Alberta's grid operator to more than double data centre power access, admits it will raise consumer prices

CanadaEnergyPolicy

The Alberta Electric System Operator (AESO) plans to more than double the "bridging load" — temporary grid power made available to new, large data centres — while acknowledging the move will push up wholesale electricity prices for other consumers. Industry estimates cited in the analysis suggest prices could climb toward $85-100/MWh, roughly triple recent levels. (Story dated July 20, 2026; included as the clearest recent account of Alberta's data centre power strategy.)

Why it matters: Alberta is positioning itself as Canada's fastest place to build data centres by leaning on its gas-heavy grid, but the tradeoff — higher bills for everyone else — is becoming an explicit, acknowledged policy choice rather than a side effect.
